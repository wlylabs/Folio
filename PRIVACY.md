## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Folio ("we") collects, uses, and protects User information when using our Platform.

## 2. Information We Collect

**Information automatically received:**
- Your public wallet address (public key) when you connect your wallet to the Platform
- On-chain transaction data (this is public blockchain data, not data we control)
- Basic technical data (browser, device, IP address) for security and analytics purposes

**Information you provide directly:**
- Article content, headlines, and images you upload when creating a Token
- Other information you voluntarily enter into Platform forms

**What we do NOT collect:**
- We never ask for or store your wallet's private key or seed phrase
- We have no access to control or move your funds

## 3. How We Use Information

Collected information is used to:
- Display Token and article content you create on the Platform
- Operate and maintain Platform functionality (indexing, charts, listings)
- Improve security and prevent misuse of the Platform
- Analyze Platform usage (if you consent to analytics cookies)

## 4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

This Platform may use cookies to:
- Remember your preferences (e.g., connected wallet)
- Analyze site usage (if applicable, e.g., Vercel Analytics/Google Analytics)

You can decline non-essential cookies via the cookie consent banner shown on your first visit, or through your browser settings.

## 5. Sharing Information with Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. Information may be shared with:
- Technical infrastructure providers (e.g., blockchain RPC providers, hosting, database services) that support Platform operations
- Legal authorities, if required by applicable legal process

Note: on-chain transaction data (wallet addresses, transaction history) is **inherently public** and can be viewed by anyone via a block explorer, regardless of our privacy practices.

## 6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect the data we store, but no system is completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your own wallet and private keys.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:
- Request access to personal data we hold about you
- Request correction or deletion of personal data (note: on-chain data cannot be deleted due to the immutable nature of blockchain)
- Withdraw consent for data use at any time

To make such a request, contact us using the details below.

## 8. Children's Data

This Platform is not intended for use by individuals under the legal age required to transact in digital assets in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the Platform.
