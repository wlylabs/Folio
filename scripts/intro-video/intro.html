What holds it up

Enforced where a component cannot skip it.

01 · Bylines

Signed, or it says so

The wallet signs a message naming this site and this address — free, no gas. Postgres refuses any listing whose byline did not sign for it. Anything published another way is labelled an unverified byline.

02 · Articles

Nothing gets rewritten

People traded on the words, so the piece that was traded on stays the piece that was traded on. Second thoughts are appended — dated, signed, and impossible to withdraw. Including by the author.

03 · Trades

Guarded before you sign

Slippage floors go on chain, so a moved price reverts instead of filling. An opening window caps each wallet and refunds the overage in the same transaction. Selling back is never closed.

Before you launch anything

Every launch here is real money.

Folio settles on Robinhood Chain. Real ETH, final trades, no practice mode and no test network. A token bought here can lose every bit of what it cost. It is not an investment, and nobody is underwriting it.
